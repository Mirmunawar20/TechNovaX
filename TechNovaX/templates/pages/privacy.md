Your privacy matters. This page explains what TechNovaX collects, why, and the choices you have.

## What we collect

- **Usage data** — if you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand which pages are popular (anonymised IP, device type, country-level location).
- **Contact and newsletter forms** — your name, email address and message, only so we can reply or send the newsletter you asked for.
- **Preferences** — your theme choice, bookmarks and cookie choice are stored in your own browser (localStorage) and never sent to us.

## Tools run in your browser

The Subnet Calculator, Password Generator and Download Time calculator run entirely on your device. Nothing you type into them is uploaded or stored.

## Cookies and advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense and similar partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits. If you choose “Essential only”, we request non-personalised ads and do not load analytics. You can manage ad personalisation at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).

## Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers that operate the site: hosting (Netlify), analytics (Google), advertising (Google AdSense) and form handling.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time. Email us using the address on the [contact section](/#contact).

## Children

TechNovaX is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “last updated” date at the top shows the latest revision.
